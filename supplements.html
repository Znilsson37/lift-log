Hydration & electrolytes

4–6 solid OTC options with real product-page URLs. Typical use: around workouts or high-sweat days. High-sodium products (e.g. LMNT) may not suit everyone — check with MD if you have hypertension or kidney disease.

OTC dietary supplement

LMNT Zero Sugar Electrolytes (Citrus Salt, 30-count)

High-sodium electrolyte stick packs (label: 1000 mg sodium, 200 mg potassium, 60 mg magnesium). Zero sugar; popular for training and low-carb diets.

Typical use note: Mix 1 stick in 16–32 oz water around training or sweaty days. High sodium — not for unrestricted daily use if salt-sensitive.

Peptides: evidence-backed, public options only

Peptide rules on this page: OTC collagen = buy links OK. FDA-approved Rx peptides (semaglutide/tirzepatide) = informational + official pages only, prescription required. Research-only / unapproved peptides = named only to explain why we will not link purchase options.

OTC dietary supplement

Hydrolyzed collagen peptides with hyaluronic acid & vitamin C; marketed for hair/skin/nails/joints. Not an anabolic steroid or fat-loss drug.

B2. FDA-approved prescription peptide medications (weight loss)

Prescription only — discuss with a physician. Links are official FDA/label or manufacturer pages, not pharmacies.

Prescription only · FDA-approved

Wegovy (semaglutide)

GLP-1 receptor agonist peptide medication for chronic weight management (and CV risk reduction in indicated adults) with diet + activity. Manufacturer: Novo Nordisk.

Label titration (informational): Injection commonly starts 0.25 mg weekly × 4 weeks, then steps up (0.5 → 1 → 1.7) toward maintenance 2.4 mg weekly (or 1.7 if needed) — physician directs.

Price: prescription / insurance — not listed as OTC

Wegovy prescribing information (manufacturer) · DailyMed label

Caveat: Boxed warning re: thyroid C-cell tumors in rodents; contraindications include personal/family MTC or MEN2. GI side effects common. Do not combine with other GLP-1s. If on TRT, coordinate your lean-mass strategy with your MD. At higher BMI these labels may apply; eligibility is your physician’s call.

Prescription only · FDA-approved

Zepbound (tirzepatide)

GIP/GLP-1 dual agonist peptide for chronic weight management (and OSA indication in eligible adults) with diet + activity. Manufacturer: Eli Lilly.

Label titration (informational): Start 2.5 mg weekly × 4 weeks (initiation only), then increase by 2.5 mg no faster than every 4 weeks; maintenance often 5, 10, or 15 mg weekly — physician directs.

Price: prescription / insurance — not listed as OTC

Zepbound manufacturer patient page · DailyMed label

Caveat: Similar serious-risk counseling as other incretins (pancreatitis, gallbladder, dehydration, etc.). Coadministration with other tirzepatide or GLP-1 products not recommended per label.

B3. Research-only / not recommended for purchase here

Not FDA-approved for bodybuilding / weight loss

Compounds such as BPC-157, TB-500, CJC-1295, ipamorelin, and similar “research peptides” are not FDA-approved for muscle building, fat loss, or recovery in healthy trainees. Quality, purity, and legality of online “research chemical” sources are unreliable and unsafe.

This package intentionally provides no buy links, vendor names, or “where to get” guidance for these. If a physician ever discusses an approved therapy, it will be through legitimate prescription channels — not underground peptide shops.
