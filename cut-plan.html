Six-pack goal: honest progress

Visible abs usually need roughly 10–12% body fat for men. Coming from a higher starting body fat, that usually takes longer than 12 weeks. Treat these 12 weeks as Phase 1: build the habits, keep your strength, and get a steady downward trend in waist and weight. After that, a planned diet break, then Phase 2.

Phase 1 milestones

Build the abs while fat comes off

Fat loss is what makes abs visible. Direct core work (2–3×/week, built into the workout program) makes them thicker, so they show sooner. Don't do hundreds of crunches. Train abs like any muscle: loaded movements, progression, then rest.

Refeeds & diet breaks

  • Refeed day (optional, from ~week 5): 1 day every 1–2 weeks at about maintenance calories. Keep protein the same, keep fat about the same, and put the extra calories into carbs. Good on a heavy training day. Mark the day as “Refeed” in the daily log and its target switches automatically.
  • Diet break: 7–14 days at estimated maintenance. Plan one after Phase 1 (around weeks 13–14). Take one earlier if strength, sleep, mood, or libido drop for 2+ weeks. Keep training and protein unchanged.
  • Refeeds and breaks are planned tools, not cheat days. They will push scale weight up 1–4 lb (water and glycogen). That's expected.

Targets (editable, overrides persist)

Changing calories, protein, or fat recalculates carbs as the remainder.

Recheck rule: update your weight and recalculate every 4 weeks or every ~10 lb lost, whichever comes first. Early loss is fastest because it includes water, so expect the rate to slow. Change things only when the 2–3 week trend stalls, not after one bad weigh-in.
