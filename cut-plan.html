Phase 1 & recheck notes
  • Six pack: abs usually show at ~10–12% body fat. From a higher start that takes more than 12 weeks, so this block is Phase 1: a steady downward trend in weight and waist, with strength holding.
  • Recheck targets every 4 weeks or every ~10 lb lost, whichever comes first. Only adjust when the 2–3 week trend stalls.
  • Early losses are faster (water). Expect ~1–1.5 lb/week later. A 1–4 lb bump after a big meal or salty day is normal.
  • After Week 12, plan a 1–2 week diet break at maintenance, then Phase 2.
