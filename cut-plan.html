Six-pack goal: honest progress

Visible abs usually need roughly 10–12% body fat for men. From a higher starting point that usually takes longer than 12 weeks, so these 12 weeks are Phase 1: build habits, keep your strength, and get a steady downward trend in waist and weight. After that comes a planned diet break, then Phase 2.

Phase 1 milestones

Fat loss reveals abs. Direct core work (2–3×/week, built into the workout) makes them thicker so they show sooner. Train them loaded and progressive, like any other muscle.

Refeeds & diet breaks

  • Refeed day (optional, from about week 5): 1 day every 1–2 weeks at roughly maintenance. Keep protein and fat the same and put the extra calories into carbs. Set the day type to “Refeed” and the target switches automatically.
  • Diet break: 7–14 days at maintenance after Phase 1 (around weeks 13–14). Take one sooner if strength, sleep, mood, or libido have been dropping for 2+ weeks.
  • Expect a 1–4 lb water bump afterward. That’s normal.

Profile & target settings

Activity guide: 1.2 sedentary · 1.375 light · 1.45 desk job + lifting 3×/wk + some physical work + walks · 1.55 moderate · 1.725 very active.

Targets (editable, overrides persist)

Changing calories, protein, or fat recalculates carbs as the remainder.

Recheck rule: update your weight and recalculate every 4 weeks or every ~10 lb lost, whichever comes first. Early losses are faster because some of it is water. Only adjust when the 2–3 week trend stalls.
