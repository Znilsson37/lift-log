Program overview

How the 12 weeks are built (tap to open)
  • 3 full-body sessions a week (A / B / C). Mon/Wed/Fri is ideal, but any 3 lunch slots work. “Today” always shows the next unfinished session this week.
  • 45–60 min: 5 min warm-up, 3–4 main lifts, 1–2 accessories, a core block (A and C, optional carry on B), and a 10–15 min cardio finisher.
  • Short on time (≤40 min): do the main lifts and the core block plus 8–10 min of cardio. Skip accessories.
  • Joint-friendly by default: goblet squat, leg press, trap-bar, and dumbbell presses come first. Barbell versions are options once they feel good. Pick any variant with the dropdown and progress tracks it.
  • Progression: once you hit the top of the rep range on all sets at the target RIR, add 5 lb (upper body) or 5–10 lb (lower body) next time.
  • RIR = reps in reserve, meaning how many more clean reps you could have done. RIR 2 means you stopped 2 reps short of failure.

⚠️ Not medical advice — tap to read

Not medical advice. Get clearance from your physician before returning to training, especially after a layoff, at 40+, or on TRT. Stop for chest pain, unusual shortness of breath, dizziness, or sharp joint pain. Soreness in weeks 1–2 is normal. Sharp pain is not.
